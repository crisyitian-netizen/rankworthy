# Evidence tiers and common claims

Tag recommendations: `[Google]` official Google (or other engine vendor) documentation · `[Study]` third-party data, usually correlational · `[Practice]` practitioner experience, unmeasured · `[Unproven]` popular claim without good evidence. When sources conflict, follow `[Google]` for Google surfaces and say so.
