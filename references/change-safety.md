# Change safety on live sites

1. **Back up first**: templates, theme files, redirects, robots.txt, sitemap settings, database or page export. Note where the backup is.
2. **Approval**: anything that changes a live page, indexing, brand claims, or spends money is drafted and approved by the owner first. Do not contact customers or third parties without approval; prepare drafts for the owner to send.
3. **Private data**: wholesale prices, internal SKUs, stock, supplier names and internal notes never appear in public pages, schema, sitemaps, llms.txt, feeds or blog posts.
4. **Language**: only target-market languages where search engines can read them. Internal-language product names or admin labels must not leak into URLs, titles or alt text.
5. **One source of facts**: shipping times, couriers, minimum order, contact details, opening hours: same wording on site, Business Profile, directories, llms.txt. Change all at once.
6. **Small batches**: change a few pages, log them, re-check, then roll out.
7. **Log** every change in the change log (measurement.md) with a re-check date.
8. **Rollback**: if metrics drop beyond normal variation after the re-check window, revert from the backup and log it.
9. **Credentials**: never ask the user to paste passwords or API keys into the chat; they set them in their own environment or tools.
